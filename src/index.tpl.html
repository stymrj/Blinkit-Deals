FAQ

Good questions

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs entirely inside your browser tab using your own Blinkit session. There is no server, no analytics and nothing is uploaded. Favourites and price history stay in your browser's local storage.

Why do I see "no results" the first time?

Blinkit prices depend on your delivery location. Open blinkit.com, set your address, then scan again. You can also type latitude and longitude in the sidebar.

Is this official?

No. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Blinkit. It only reads pages your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Blinkit can change its internal APIs without notice. The parser reads responses generically to tolerate small changes, but if a scan ever returns nothing, the tool will tell you what to check.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After every scan the tool remembers each product's price in your browser. On the next scan, anything new gets a NEW badge, and anything cheaper than before gets PRICE ↓.

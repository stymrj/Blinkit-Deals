Blinkit Deal Scout

Scan Blinkit across dozens of keywords and collections, rank by real savings, and build a smart basket.

⚡ Deal Radar

↑ Drag the yellow button to your bookmarks bar

Install

  1. Desktop: drag ⚡ Deal Radar to your bookmarks bar. Open blinkit.com, set your delivery location, click the bookmark, press Scan deals.
  2. Mobile: tap Copy for mobile, create any bookmark, edit it and paste as the URL. On blinkit.com type the bookmark name in the address bar and pick it.

Everything runs in your browser using your own Blinkit session. Nothing is sent anywhere else. The script is self-contained (no remote code loading).
